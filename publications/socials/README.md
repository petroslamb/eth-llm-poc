# Distribution Guide

How to distribute the eip-verify publications across platforms.

> **Note:** All posts contain `[KILLER_PIECE_LINK]` / `[KILLER_PIECE_SUBSTACK_URL]` placeholders.
> Replace these with the actual Substack URL after publishing the killer piece (no email send). Also replace `[V2_LINK]` and `[GOV_LINK]` in `substack_notes.md` with the published Substack URLs for those posts.

---

## Distribution Order (stagger across 2–3 days)

| Day | Platform | Post |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Substack (no email) | Publish killer piece, pin as featured |
| Day 1 | Substack Notes | Notes promoting the killer piece |
| Day 1 | Twitter/X | Thread or single tweet |
| Day 2 | ethresear.ch | Technical post (adapted version) |
| Day 2 | LinkedIn | Professional summary |
| Day 3 | Hacker News | Submit link + author comment |
| Day 3 | Reddit | Tailored text posts per subreddit |

---

## Timing Guidelines

- **HN:** Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11 AM ET
- **Reddit:** Tuesday–Wednesday, morning US time
- **LinkedIn:** Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10 AM local business time
- **Twitter/X:** Post when your followers are active; pin the thread

---

## Platform Rules to Follow

- **HN:** Neutral title (no clickbait). Disclose authorship in first comment. Use `Show HN:` if promoting the tool, plain submit if promoting the essay. [Guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html)
- **Reddit:** Tailored text post per subreddit. Do NOT cross-post identical link-drops. [Spam policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam)
- **ethresear.ch:** Technical, information-rich. Not Substack voice. [Posting guide](https://ethresear.ch/t/read-this-before-posting/8)
- **LinkedIn:** Framework-first. Lead with the pattern, not the project.
